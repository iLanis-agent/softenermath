Boundary: illustrative arithmetic only Softener Math keeps labeled-norm arithmetic only. Hardness bands, the 75-gallon person-day, the two-thirds efficient dose and the ppm conversion are labeled norms, not measurements of your water, your resin or your household. Test your water with a real kit, follow the maker's capacity and salt charts over anything this page says, and ask a water professional about your plumbing.

The hardness truth

Grains per gallon have names. Ten grains per gallon reads hard honestly - 171.0 ppm - and the band edges were never fuzzy.

The capacity truth

The 32,000-grain rating is the max-salt figure. At the efficient dose the same unit holds two-thirds of that, so four people on 10-grain water regen every 10.7 days at the label and every 7.1 days honestly.

The salt truth

Salt per regeneration times regenerations per month is the real grocery bill: 12 pounds 2.8 times a month is 33.6 pounds - 0.8 bags of 40 honestly.
